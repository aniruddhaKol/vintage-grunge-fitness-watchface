## Font-generated assets

`scripts/make-assets.ps1` uses installed Impact to generate individual glyph sprites. No standalone font binary was found in this repository. [Microsoft's font redistribution FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/fonts/font-faq) distinguishes permitted text images from converting fonts into individual glyph bitmap fonts. Confirm the font source and appropriate redistribution rights, or arrange a separately reviewed replacement, before further distribution.
